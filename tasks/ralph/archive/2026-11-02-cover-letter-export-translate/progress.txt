  - New `src/app/api/cover-letters/[id]/translate/route.ts` (+ route.test.ts): POST { targetLanguage }, maxDuration 60.
    - Checks, in order:
      1. 401;
      2. 415 (JSON only);
      3. 400 (Zod: en/fr/de/it);
      4. 404 for a bad uuid, a missing letter or another user's letter (session read plus explicit user_id);
      5. 422 above the size guard;
      6. 422 above the AI limits (120 segments, 12,000 characters);
      7. 422 `{ error: 'empty' }` for a letter with no text;
      8. only then `enforceAiRateLimit`.
    - Errors and insert:
      - a provider error gives 502;
      - a provider rate limit gives 429;
      - an incomplete rebuild (missing part, or any overflow) gives 502;
      - otherwise one insert under the session user, title "<title> (XX)" cut to fit 255.
    - It never writes the original letter or job_applications.
  - New `translate/letter-segments.ts` (+ test): splits the stored letter into segments with US-002's DOM-free allowlist parser, each written in a mini-markup (`<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<br>`). Rebuilding the reply:
    - the structure comes from the original, never from the reply;
    - the reply is re-parsed with the allowlist;
    - each run goes through `sanitizeUntrustedPlainText` with its own length as the limit (nothing is ever cut) and is escaped;
