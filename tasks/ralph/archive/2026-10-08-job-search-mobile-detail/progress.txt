Decision (FR-2): history.pushState with no URL, so the on-screen control calls history.back() and a popstate listener closes the detail. Before relying on it, Next 15.5.26 app-router.js was read:
- its patched pushState only copies __NA and the route tree when no URL is given;
- on popstate it restores the same href and tree, so nothing remounts.
Rejected: a `?job=` query or a /dashboard/jobs/[id] route. Deep links are out of scope, and a route change would remount the page and lose the in-memory results.

Decision (layout): keep the list mounted and hide it with CSS while the detail is open. Unmounting would lose the loaded pages, and the hidden IntersectionObserver sentinel cannot fire. The header and filters are hidden too, so a filter change cannot reload results under an open detail.

Known limits recorded by this story (code-reviewer Minors 2, 3, 6; not blocking):
- Opening a detail at 375 and resizing past 768 leaves a stale open state: one back press does nothing visible. Opening a detail, following a sidebar link, then pressing back lands on a detail entry with the layout remounted closed.
- Ghost tap: on a fast double tap, the second tap can land on the detail content that just appeared under the finger.
- The FR-3 ancestor guard in the specs checks transform, filter and contain, but not will-change, perspective or backdrop-filter.
