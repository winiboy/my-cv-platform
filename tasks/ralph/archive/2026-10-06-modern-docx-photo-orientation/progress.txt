Decision (AC-01, FR-5): the orientation is written as the picture's OOXML transform (a:xfrm rot + flipH/flipV via the
docx library's ImageRun transformation), not by rotating pixels. Measured first on hand-edited XML in Word for all 8
orientations (zone |Δ| 0.98–1.75 vs the EXIF-applied expected image), which disproved the PRD's BLOCKER condition.
Rejected: pixel rotation — needs an image dependency (owner decision per the PRD) and re-encodes the photo.
Word applies the flip before the rotation; 5 = flipH + 270°, 7 = flipH + 90°. Orientations 5–8 lay the picture out as
220 × zone width and offset it by ±(W − H)/2 so the turned box is the zone. The swapped-axes srcRect mapping relies on
the centred cover crop; docx-modern.ts says so.
