- **Header picture present.** Current Modern DOCX (main 3d4a499): `word/header1.xml` holds a `wp:anchor` with behindDoc="1" at page (0,0), extent 2268220 × 10692130 EMU (sidebar width × 16838 twips). The sidebar cell fill is 0D0DA5. This holds for the 1-, 2- and 3-page files.
- **Word PDF export is uniform.** On every page of the 1-, 2- and 3-page files, the bottom 40 × 238 px of the sidebar are 9520/9520 #0d0da5, and the bottom 60 px at x=100 are #0d0da5 from y 1063 to 1122. No band appears in print or PDF.
- **Word's own Print Layout render (Pages(n).EnhMetaFileBits) is also uniform**, down to y=1121. This route is the print path and cannot show on-screen dimming: with the header active it renders identically.
- **Where the cell fill ends**, measured on copies with the header reference removed:

  | File | Page | Fill ends at | Gap to page foot |
  |---|---|---|---|

  | 3 pages | 3 (last) | y 1089 | 33 px |

  Only the header picture paints these gaps.
- **On-screen evidence (owner).**
  - The owner's screenshot of 2026-10-11, Word Print Layout of a 2-page Modern CV, shows a lighter blue band at the foot of the sidebar on pages 1 and 2. That is exactly where the measured gaps are.
  - On 2026-10-11 the owner opened two files sent by the agent in Word Print Layout: 1-actuel-2pages.docx (current code) and 2-correction-P2-2pages.docx (probe P2, below). The owner replied "le 2e fichier est bon, lance la correction P2".
  - Named limit: the owner did not explicitly confirm the band on the current-code file. It is inferred from the original screenshot together with "the second file is good".
- **The owner's own file.** It was not provided, so whether it contains header1.xml is not recorded. The lighter (not white) colour of the band rules out a pre-#88 export, which would show white.

Cause: Word draws header content dimmed in Print Layout while the body is being edited. The full-page sidebar picture lives in the header, added by #88. Where the sidebar cell's shading stops, at the last line on each page and up to 33 px short on the last page, the user sees the dimmed header picture as a lighter band. Print and PDF are not dimmed, which is why the COM-PDF method never showed it.
