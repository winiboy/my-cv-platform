Decision:
- No font on the serverless runtime has U+2010–2012: neither the substitutes' latin subsets nor @sparticuz/chromium's Open Sans (cmaps read from the installed files). So the substitute itself was extended with a cmap-only supplement, and the text was left alone. This keeps the BLOCKER about rewriting text unreached.
