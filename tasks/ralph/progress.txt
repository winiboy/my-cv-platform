Cause: Word draws header content dimmed in Print Layout while the body is being edited. The full-page sidebar picture lives in the header, added by #88. Where the sidebar cell's shading stops, at the last line on each page and up to 33 px short on the last page, the user sees the dimmed header picture as a lighter band. Print and PDF are not dimmed, which is why the COM-PDF method never showed it.
