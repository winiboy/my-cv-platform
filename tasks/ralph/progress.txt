Decision (orchestrator): the size guard is a resource bound, separate from the API input limits. The editor saves straight to Supabase without those limits, and the export must not refuse letters it accepted before.
Decision: one render-slot pool of 2 for both PDF routes, as the PRD's shared-pool risk describes.
