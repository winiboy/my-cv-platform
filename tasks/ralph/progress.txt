## US-001: The resume PDF fidelity tests compare content, not pdf.js spacing

Scope:
- Scope check: PASS
- Changed paths (git diff --numstat; story diff sha256 prefix f2b6fe90e211c267, over the tracked e2e diff plus the two new files):
  - New `e2e/fixtures/pdf-text.ts` (24): `comparablePageText(text) = text.replace(/\s+/g, '')`.
    - Its doc comment explains why whitespace is not compared: how pdf.js splits a line into items depends on glyph placement, so letter-spaced headings extract as "V I S U A L" from one print and "VISUAL" from another.
    - It also states what is still caught: any added, removed, changed or moved character, and swapped words.
    - Its one blind spot is a word boundary moving with no other character change ("ab cd" vs "a bcd"), which the PRD's FR-2 and Risks accept.
  - New `src/test/e2e-pdf-text.test.ts` (46), 7 cases.
    - Compare equal: the observed glyph-by-glyph heading against whole words; texts differing only in kind or amount of whitespace.
    - Compare different: one changed character; one added and one removed; reordered characters; two swapped words in the observed letter-spaced shape; a case change; `-` vs `–`.
    - It lives in `src/test/` because vitest collects only `src/**/*.test.ts`, and a `*.test.ts` under `e2e/` would be collected by Playwright. A `src` test importing from `e2e` has precedent in `src/lib/resume-text-opacity.test.ts`.
  - `e2e/resume-pdf-route.spec.ts` (14/4):
    - `expectSamePdf` compares `comparablePageText` of each page, still per page index.
    - Three comments that said the page text must be identical are corrected.
    - Page count, page size, the A4 check, no-print-chrome and the modern band are unchanged.
- Not touched: `e2e/fixtures/pdf.ts` (`readPdf`) and its other users — `cover-letter-pdf-export`, `cover-letter-template-language`, and `resume-pdf-download-button` with its own exact `expectSamePdf`. No product code. No version change, no push or merge.

Decision:

Open owner decisions:
- **The flake did not reproduce** in any of the 210 repeat runs or the two full runs, so this evidence shows the comparison is sound and nothing broke, not that the original mismatch is gone. The fix rests on the unit test and the diagnosis (whitespace-only differences in the recorded failures). The first-attempt CI E2E result on the PR is the remaining evidence the PRD asks for.
