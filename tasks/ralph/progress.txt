Decision (owner, 2026-10-13, asked in chat): FR-4 says "network limited to the app's own origin". The renderer may also make GET requests to fonts.googleapis.com (/css, /css2, stylesheets only) and fonts.gstatic.com (/s/, fonts only).
- Reason: the Preview loads the font picker's families from Google Fonts. Blocking them would print a different font, line breaks and page count from the user's own print.
- No cookie or resume data goes to those hosts, and only the bare origin is sent as referrer.
- Rejected alternative: self-hosting the fonts, which would change the root layout and is out of this PRD's scope.

Named limits:
- WebP photos: the photo bound (the same as the DOCX's boundedPhoto) accepts jpeg/jpg/png/gif/bmp only, so a WebP photo shown in the Preview is missing from the PDF, as it is from the DOCX. This is a recorded fidelity limitation (.claude/rules/exports.md), not a pass. Chromium itself could draw WebP.
- The session-refresh path (session expiring within 150 s) has no runtime test (security INFO-D).
- The defence in depth is proven at unit level only. The sanitizer removes every malicious vector before request blocking has anything to block, and the blocking itself is covered by the policy and route-guard unit tests.

Open owner decisions recorded by this story (for US-002 / US-003):
- Vercel rejects request bodies over 4.5 MB before the route runs, while the route accepts about 10 MiB. A large photo would fail in production with Vercel's own 413. US-002 must either fit the limit or report that failure in the user's language.
- NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL is fixed at build time. On a preview deployment it may point to production, and Deployment Protection may refuse the renderer. US-003 must record which origin rendered the PDF (PRD BLOCKER condition).
- Chromium on Vercel: playwright-core ships no browser and the sandbox is usually unavailable there. US-003 swaps only browser.ts (@sparticuz/chromium, sandbox false) and must record that it did.

Environment notes:
- Windows; Playwright suites ran one at a time. The DOCX hash script was run on its own and deleted before any check.
