Decision:
- **One status line, always there.** The status is always on its own line under the title, as in the CV editor. Its 16 px line is reserved before any status, so the title never moves when the first status appears.
- **The CV editor's time format, exactly.** "Same format as the CV editor" is the binding part of AC-05, so en shows "04:10 PM" rather than the PRD example's "3:51 PM".

Open owner decisions:
- **Header height at xl:** the header is 69 px at xl, about 4 px taller than before by code review's estimate (not measured). The two-line title column is taller than the buttons, so the buttons sit about 2 px lower. Their order and row are unchanged.
- **Room for the title at 1280:** about 230 px. The title is now cut only at the button group; more room would mean changing the buttons, which this PRD rules out.
- **en time:** "04:10 PM" carries a leading zero, as in the CV editor. Dropping it means changing both editors to `hour: 'numeric'`.
- Follow-ups noted by ui-expert, both older than this story:
  - the title input's accessible name is its placeholder, "Lettre sans titre", even with a typed title; a translated `aria-label` would fix it;
  - the amber "unsaved" text is about 3.2:1 on white in both editors, below AA for 12 px; `amber-700` would give about 5.0:1.
