## US-002: The job-applications i18n spec waits for what it asserts
Status: PASS
Priority: 2

Scope:
- Scope check: PASS
- Changed paths (git diff --numstat; story diff sha256 prefix d9349cfd1ac5d0d6): `e2e/job-applications-i18n.spec.ts` (83/23) only.
  - **New `afterQueuedTimers(page)`:** queues a 10 ms timer in the page and awaits it.
    - It is an ordering barrier, not a sleep. Under the HTML timer rules it runs after any timer already queued with a delay of 10 ms or less.
    - It is used only where the app finishes an action from a zero-delay timer that nothing on screen shows, after an assertion that proves that timer has been queued.
  - **Quick add:**
    - the test waits until the title is focused after the modal opens;
    - `settleInvalidSubmitFocus(title)` runs after each failed submit;
    - new `toHaveValue` checks after the fills make text in the wrong field fail at the fill;

  - **Drags:** `pointerPickUpAndMove` and `mouseDrag` take the origin column and wait (`expectOver`) for the origin to show as "over" before moving, and for the target before releasing.
  - **Keyboard drag:** `expectColumnsMeasured` is renamed `expectReadyForArrowKeys` and adds `afterQueuedTimers`.

Decision (the diagnosis):
- **Cause 1, quick add.** Playwright's `fill` focuses the field, then inserts the text in a separate step (playwright-core 1.63). Two timers can move focus to the title between those steps:
  - the modal's 50 ms `setFocus('job_title')` after opening (`quick-add-modal.tsx` L99–107);
  - react-hook-form's post-submit `_focusError(); setTimeout(_focusError)`.
  - Forcing that focus move reproduced both CI symptoms of PR #121: the company field left empty, showing "obligatoire" instead of "too long"; and "not a url" typed into the title.
  - The form's validation is correct: all three messages come from one pass, 36/36, including under CPU throttling ×6 and ×12.
- **Cause 2, mouse drag.** dnd-kit's end handler needs `sensorContext.current.active`, which is set in a layout effect after the drag start commits. A release handled before that dispatches DragEnd with no event, so no drop announcement and no PATCH: the `:1142` symptom.
- **Cause 3, keyboard drag.** The keyboard sensor adds its keydown listener from a zero-delay timer queued at pick-up, so an ArrowRight pressed before it runs is lost.
- The CI trace artifacts of run 41999923969 were not downloaded (downloads need the owner's approval), so it is not proven which quick-add timer fired in CI. Both are now covered.

Open owner decisions:
- **Existing UX quirk** (code-reviewer, out of scope under FR-3): a keyboard user who opens quick add and types within 50 ms can lose the first keystrokes. A user who clicks into company within 50 ms gets focus pulled back to the title.
- Optional (code-reviewer, minor):
  - The `afterQueuedTimers` docblock could state that the caller must first prove, with an assertion, that the target timer has been queued.
  - The `expect.poll` around the full-log equality checks is redundant after the `lastAnnouncement` poll.
- **Other one-off failures** listed in the PRD's Context stay out of scope: `layout-persistence:172`, `modern-print-sidebar-full-page:365`, `cover-letter-editor-spacing:150`, `resume-pdf-route:333`. None recurred in the 4 full runs of this initiative.
