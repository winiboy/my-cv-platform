Decision (implementer, accepted by code-reviewer): an invalid offer does not block the CV; it is ignored and only its field names are logged.
Decision: the offer uses Save's schema with two stricter rules: status is dropped, and job_url must be http(s). Save itself is unchanged.
Decision: the application uses the listing's own fields, like Save, not the text fetched from the external page.
Known debt (mandated by FR-2, code-reviewer minor): the dedupe keeps Save's `.single()` lookup. With two existing same-URL rows, or a read error, nothing is found and another row is created; concurrent clicks can race. A unique constraint is out of scope.
