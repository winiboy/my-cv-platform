  utility — was not under observation. `showControls` defaults to true, so a
  real user prints with controls ON and `print:hidden` is the sole mechanism
  keeping sliders out of their PDF; the spec unchecked the controls, which
  removes them from the DOM entirely. Deleting `print:hidden` anywhere would
  have left both baselines byte-identical and the suite green.

Harness limitation found during this story:
- `pnpm test:visual` and `pnpm test:e2e` cannot run from a `.claude/worktrees/`
  checkout. Both configs carry `testIgnore: ['**/.claude/**']` and Playwright
  matches it against the absolute path, so an agent worktree matches its own
  ignore rule and reports "No tests found". All verification for this story ran
  in the main checkout.
