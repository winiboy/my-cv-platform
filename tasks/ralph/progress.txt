Decision:
- **One migration for all fifteen ids.** The PRD asks for the set to be widened once, in US-001.
  - Until each template's story lands, no surface offers its id.
  - A row holding one through a direct write renders as modern in every output, Word included.

  - Six cards measure 1198.3 px at 375 px (fr, en and it on the page; 1208.0 in the de modal), so a fixed 1100 cannot hold.
  - The ceiling is now 220 px per option (1320 for six). The modal's 1440 px ceiling is per row (650/3, which is 650 for six options, unchanged).
  - Every per-card assertion is unchanged: at most 200 px, at most 340 at 768, no split words.
- **Shorter descriptions.** The fr and it descriptions are shortened so the card stays within the 200 px card ceiling: "Format suisse, idéal après un CFC" and "Formato svizzero, ideale dopo un AFC".

- **Dates follow the mockup:** `MM.YYYY` in every locale, read from the stored string, so no time zone can shift the month.

Open owner decisions:
- **Word font:** Word draws the document in a serif fallback when Inter is not installed. That comes from the shared `APP_BODY_FONT`, so it affects every template. A fontTable hint (`w:family="swiss"`) would fix it; that is a follow-up PRD.
- **Word skill rows:** wrapped lines start under the label, because Word cannot measure the label width. Word's heading squares are taller than wide; this is documented in `resume-graphics.ts`.
- **PDF page 2:** there is no top margin at the start of page 2, because `globals.css` sets `@page { margin: 0 }` for every template. The PDF pipeline is out of scope here.
- **Glued text in the PDF:** the five existing templates very likely run adjacent columns together in their PDF text (for example language and level, or position and date). This was found by reading their markup and was not measured; it hurts copy-paste and ATS parsing. It is not fixed here and is a candidate for its own PRD.
- **Untranslated panel text:** the section panel's "Edit Sidebar" title and its "drag and drop" text are not translated in any template.
- **Shared photo helpers:** `docx-swiss-vocational.ts` imports them from `docx-modern.ts`. Moving them to `docx-helpers.ts` is advised before the other nine generators are written.
