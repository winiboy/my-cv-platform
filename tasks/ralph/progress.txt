Decision (a): hand-written focus trap on the existing div.fixed.inset-0 root (pattern of quick-add-modal.tsx), not a native <dialog>. Toasts render in a fixed z-50 container; a showModal() dialog sits in the top layer and makes the page inert, so a toast shown while the modal is open would be hidden and unclickable. Keeping the root also leaves stacking, the template picker and the phone layout unchanged.
Decision (b): focus starts on the close button (not the first field), so a phone's keyboard does not cover the modal on open; when a stage change removes the focused control, focus returns to the close button.
Decision (c): Escape calls the same onClose as the X button, also while a request is in flight: the modal closes, focus returns, the request finishes in the background (create flow still redirects on success, as with X today).
Decision (d): focus returns to returnFocusRef when given (Job Search: its buttons are disabled during the job fetch and the resume selector may open in between), otherwise to the element focused at open; restored on close or unmount if still on the page.
Decision (e), beyond AC5's close button: the patch checkboxes, "Tout sélectionner", "Tout désélectionner" and "Annuler" got the close button's focus-visible ring. Their only indicator was the global outline-ring/50 (alpha 0.5); the story promises a keyboard-accessible dialog and the trap makes Tab the normal way through it. focus-visible only, so nothing changes for mouse users (confirmed by the before/after pixel diff).
