Decision:
- A radiogroup of native radios, not a tablist: its panels would also be tab panels at 1280 and above, where there are no tabs. Radios also don't collide with the specs' `getByRole('button', { name: 'Preview' })` for the header's Preview button.
- Hide, not unmount, so rich-text state survives. The preview remounts on entering Aperçu, so it fits at once; zoom therefore resets to fitted on each entry.
- The scroll position is not kept across a switch: below xl `<main>` scrolls, and Édition starts from the top again. The PRD does not require it.
