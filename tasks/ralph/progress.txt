  - **New `src/lib/cover-letter-from-job/offer-page-address.ts`:** `companyAddressFromOfferPage(html)`.
    - It reads every `<script type="application/ld+json">` block, by a forward `indexOf` scan, with only ASCII A–Z lower-cased, so the positions match the original HTML.
    - It finds every `JobPosting`: a single node, an array or `@graph`, with `@type` as a string, a list, or a full name such as `https://schema.org/JobPosting`.

  - **New `offer-text-address.ts`:** the AI's answer is accepted only when it matches the offer text's own structure.
    - The text the AI was given (first 15,000 characters) is cut into segments before any cleaning, on:
      - line breaks and their entities;
      - block/line HTML tags and U+200B;
      - tab;
      - `, ; : | / · •` and full-width forms;
      - dashes with spaces on both sides.
    - Segments are compared in NFC, lower-cased, with whitespace collapsed, a trailing full stop removed, and leading bullets, list numbers and Markdown markers removed.
    - The street must equal a whole segment, and the next segment must equal "postcode locality". An optional country prefix on the postcode in the text is allowed, and so is one P.O.-box segment in between.
    - Street rules:
      - it needs a house number (digits, a letter, `bis`/`ter`, or a range) and a street-type word (`STREET_TYPE_WORDS` fr/it/en, `STREET_TYPE_SUFFIXES` German);
      - it is refused when it holds the postcode, the company name, only company-name words, a legal form (`LEGAL_FORMS`), a P.O.-box phrase, a floor/building/department word, or a second number.
    - Postcode: `^(?:[A-Za-z]{1,2}-)?\d{4,5}$`.
    - Locality rules:
      - a letter and no digit;
      - at most 4 tokens;
      - no brackets, no sentence full stop, no trailing country, no company name or legal form, no partial overlap with company-name words of 3+ letters;
      - no word from `NOT_A_LOCALITY_WORDS`;
      - further tokens must be capitalised or be place particles.
    - A non-Swiss country is kept only when it is the next segment and is in `KNOWN_COUNTRIES`; otherwise the line is dropped.
    - `offer-page-address.ts` now exports `postalAddressParts`, `formatPostalAddressParts` and `SWITZERLAND`. The page path (US-001) gives the same output.
  - **Stub:** `AI_TEST_STUB_OFFER_TEXT_ADDRESS` ("Rue du Lac 5", "1201", "Genève", "CH") is answered only when asked, whatever the text says, so the route's real check decides.

Decision:
- **The check rests on the offer text's own structure, not on the AI's answer.** Rounds 1–4 checked the AI's own string and each left another way in. Whole-segment equality on the text's lines, blocks and separators closes that class.
- **A street must prove it is a street.** A house number plus a street-type word. Floors, P.O. boxes, departments, references and company names with a number fail by construction.

Open owner decisions:
- **Known false rejects (the field stays empty; the user types the address):**
  - streets without a listed type word ("Steinenvorstadt 30", "Schipfe 2", "Bollwerk 4", "1 Main St");
  - date-named streets ("Rue du 31-Décembre 47", "Avenue du 1er-Mars 5") and a spaced range ("Rue de la Gare 1 - 3");
  - official slash names ("Biel/Bienne", "Domat/Ems"); "/" is a segment separator;
  - "Collina d'Oro", "Bern 22", "Paris Cedex 08";
  - a postcode and town on separate lines;
  - "Rue du Lac 5, 2e étage, 1201 Genève";
  - HTML named entities such as `&egrave;` in a stored offer text.
  - Biel/Bienne is the most visible of these. Accepting a slash between two letters would need its own pass.
- **Known false accepts, all contrived (code review round 7: class b):** words the offer itself writes after the street or the town in the same segment, which the AI copies against the prompt:
  - a title or department ("Bahnhofstrasse 1 HR");
  - an unnumbered German floor ("Bahnhofstrasse 1 Erdgeschoss");
  - a bracketed note on the street;
  - a fragment of the company name after the street;
  - a capitalised non-place word after the town ("Zürich Telefon");
  - a name the offer writes in the same segment as the street;
  - "Engineering 2", "Technopark 2"-like segments right before the postcode;
  - "Basel-Coop".
  - In every case the stored text comes from the offer and is shown in the editable field. Cheap follow-ups exist: share the locality's refusal list and bracket rule with the street, add the floor compounds and contact words.
